# VENUES

!!! abstract "Grain"
    One venue. Its phone, email and address live on its [CONTACTS](@uritp-people-table-contacts) card.

## Fields

!!! data "catalog"

## A venue is not a person record

A box office or a building desk needs a line on a contact sheet, so it owns a contact card. It is never entered as a fake person.
