# APP_TABLES

⛔ **SUPERSEDED 2026-10-23 by [Target_Tables](@uritp-people-table-target-tables).**

This page described a table that does not exist and never did. URITP People built the app catalog's table half as `Target_Tables`, with `fm_BaseTableID`, `fm_BaseTableName`, `isPresent` and `LastSeenAt`, and Michael ruled the built name stands (D-049). The standard and `APP_CatalogRefresh` now say `Target_Tables`.

Kept rather than deleted because D-043 and several pages referred to `APP_TABLES` by name, and a reader arriving from one of them needs to land somewhere that explains the rename instead of a 404.

What moved with it: the `ClickUpFed` flag and the notes this page claimed get typed were never created either, so they are not owed anywhere. `IMPORT_SOURCES` pointing a source's target at a catalog record now means a `Target_Tables` record.
