# Projection law

🔒 **LOCKED 2026-08-08 (Michael): there are NO manual edits to the projection layer. Ever.**

> *"let's just not plan to do ManualEdits and have a formatting engine that is robust enough to get what I need done."*

## The rule

**WEEKS · WORKDAYS · EVENTS_workday are 100% derived.** Delete-and-rebuild, always, on any trigger. No flags, no preservation pass, no reconciliation, no verify-before-commit step. **Nothing is ever lost because nothing is ever authored there.**

The `ManualEdit` flag is **killed before it shipped** — and killing it removes the whole machine it needed: a preservation pass on rebuild, a divergence report, an accept/discard decision per row. That machinery existed only to protect edits that now cannot happen.

⭐ **Why this is the right call and not just the simpler one:** a disposable table with edits in it is not disposable, it is a second source of truth wearing a temporary costume. Same species as the `autoGenerated` flag we killed on the legacy duplicates, and the convenience-copy dates, and the second-claimant highlight fields. **Fourth instance in one session.**

## What this OBLIGATES the engine to do

The pressure that creates spot-edits is real. Refusing the edits means the engine has to cover the cases, so they are named here rather than discovered under deadline:

| Pressure | Must be solved by |
|---|---|
| "this one event needs different styling on this one page" | STYLES row or a CALENDAR_EMPHASIS row — a rule, not a keystroke |
| "this week needs a page break here" | `WEEKS.PAGE_BREAK` is generated; the RULE that sets it belongs on the preset |
| "this day is too crowded to fit" | overflow behaviour in the layout, not a hand-trimmed cell |
| "the name is too long for the cell" | `UseShortTitle` + a length-aware display calc |
| "drop this event from this one printout" | a preset FILTER (the legacy agenda already did exactly this to hide rehearsals) |
| "a typo in an event name" | fix it in **ClickUp**, reimport. ClickUp authors. |

🔴 **The last row is the load-bearing one.** Every "just let me fix it on the page" is really a request to author data in the render layer, which is the architecture we locked on 08-07: **ClickUp authors, FileMaker renders.**

## Consequences, stated plainly

- ✅ Rebuild is dumb and safe: `Delete All Records` in scope, regenerate. No branching.
- ✅ Reprinting an old PRINT_SESSIONS row is honest: it renders today's truth, and the paper was never authoritative.
- ✅ No verify-before-commit pass to build or maintain.
- ⚠️ **A formatting need with no rule behind it becomes a BUILD task, not a workaround.** That is the trade: slower on the one-off, and the app never accumulates untracked hand-fixes.
- ⚠️ If a genuinely unrulable one-off ever appears, the answer is **export the PDF and edit the PDF** — outside the system, where it cannot pretend to be data.
