# MAW Previs

## What it is

- Blender previs for URITP shows in SPAC
- Block against rehearsal video, then light photorealistically
- Blocking and lighting never touch each other
- Venue and stand-ins are built once; each show adds only its own
- Fixtures come from the Vectorworks plot; Eos drives them

## The pages

1. [[Architecture](@previs-architecture)]: what lives where. Everything else depends on it.
2. [[Blocking Method](@previs-blocking-method)]: how performers, cubes and props move and get timed.
