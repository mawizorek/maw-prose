
## Coming out

A **production archive** for each show, curated and submitted by you:

- the final plots, as hung, not as drawn
- the show file
- the patch list
- run sheets
- channel hookup, network and device arrangement, tech notes

As-hung versus as-drawn is the entire point of the archive. A designer's file tells the next person what somebody wanted. Yours tells them what was actually in the air, which is the only version worth inheriting.

After every production the lighting and cable inventories get updated again. Beginning and end, both, every time.

And you maintain the HE handbook. Which is what this package is, so if you are reading it and something in here is wrong, that is now your job.
