# Head Electrician

*The map for this package. Written for whoever takes the job next, an assistant stepping up mid-season, or a designer who needs to know what the HE is actually responsible for. Content last checked: 2026-07-29.*

The Head Electrician is the contact person for everything lighting and electrical on a production, and the custodian of the lighting and cable inventory while that production is running. Two real notes exist. The rest of this package is a skeleton: the phase notes are the ones that carry the craft, and they are empty until someone who has done the job dictates them.

## What is here

**[The job](./the-job.md)** is what you are on the hook for: who you report to, who you supervise, what you have to enroll in and attend, and what "present" means at each stage of a production. Real content, taken from the 2026 duties and responsibilities.

**[Deliverables](./deliverables.md)** is what you owe and when. Paperwork in, paperwork out, and the production archive you hand over at the end. Real content, same source.

## The phase notes

These follow the spine the existing handbook site already uses, because that is how the job is actually retrieved: nobody opens a handbook on a Tuesday, they open it the morning of load-in. All six are **empty on purpose**, headings only. The headings are the questions.

- **[Preseason](./preseason.md)**, before there is a show to run
- **[Paperwork prep](./paperwork-prep.md)**, plot in hand, nothing hung yet
- **[Load-in](./load-in.md)**, hang, cable, dimmer check, focus
- **[Tech](./tech.md)**, the week everything breaks
- **[Show run](./show-run.md)**, maintenance and the ops
- **[Strike](./strike.md)**, out and back to storage

**[Inventory](./inventory.md)** sits outside the phase sequence because it is a standing duty rather than a moment. Also empty.

## Where the existing content lives

There is already a handbook, and it is a Google Site: `sites.google.com/view/uritplx`. Its structure is the source of the spine above, and it carries a useful legend worth knowing before you read it: **blue text is a task the HE has to complete, italics are called-out notes, pink means go research it.** It also opens with a disclaimer that is honest and worth repeating here: this is not a step-by-step manual, and some of the job you will only learn by asking someone who has done it.

Other source material sits in Dropbox: the previous handbooks, the theatre rig reference, and the LX and sound resources. None of it has been brought into this package yet.

## What "done" looks like

The six phase notes written, plus inventory. That is the whole scope, and it has an end, which is deliberate: an open-ended documentation project is the kind that sits half-finished for weeks.
