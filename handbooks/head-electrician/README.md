# Head Electrician

*Written for whoever takes the job next, an assistant stepping up mid-season, or a designer who needs to know what the HE owns. Source: `URITP Duties and Responsibilities`, version 2026; checked 2026-07-30.*

The Head Electrician is the contact person for everything lighting and electrical on a production, and the custodian of the lighting and cable inventory while that assignment runs.

- **[The job](./the-job.md)** — who you report to, who you supervise, what you have to enroll in and attend, and what "present" means at each stage.
- **[Deliverables](./deliverables.md)** — what you owe and when. Paperwork in, paperwork out, and the production archive at the end.

## What is not here

How a load-in or a tech week actually runs — that is [guides/production-phases](../../guides/production-phases/). Electrics, audio, video, scenic and stage management all live through the same load-in, so it is written once and cited.

Venue specifics — dimmer rack locations, circuit numbering, the dead circuit nobody remembers — belong to the room: [Smith Theatre electrics](../../venues/smith-theatre/electrics.md), which is still empty.

## The handbook that already exists

There is a published HE handbook and it is a Google Site: `sites.google.com/view/uritplx`. Its legend is worth knowing before you read it — blue text is a task the HE has to complete, italics are called-out notes, pink means go research it. It opens with a disclaimer worth repeating: it is not a step-by-step manual, and some of this job you will only learn by asking someone who has done it.

Other source material sits in Dropbox — previous handbooks, the theatre rig reference, the LX and sound resources. None of it has been brought in yet.
