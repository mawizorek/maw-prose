## Focus

**The one thing worth writing down now: split the room into three jobs, not two.** One person drives the console. One person owns the focus charts and interprets them, and that is a distinct job from running the board, because reading a designer's chart while also patching and pushing faders is how a focus session stalls. The HE floats between them: making sure the console has what it needs, and that the chart reading is unambiguous. If the designer's charts arrive ahead of the session, whoever is going to interpret them reads them cold and brings their questions to the designer **before** anyone is up a ladder.

*That split is how it was run on a 2026 mainstage with charts delivered in advance. It is a practice, not a policy.*
