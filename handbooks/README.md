# Handbooks

*How you hold a role over time. One package per role, read by whoever holds it. Content last checked: 2026-07-30.*

A handbook answers *what am I on the hook for, and what do I owe*. It belongs to a **person in a role**. It is not how to perform a task in the moment: that is a [guide](../guides/), and the production phases live there so that every role can cite the same load-in rather than each keeping its own copy.

## The roles

**[Head Electrician](./head-electrician/)** — the contact for everything lighting and electrical on a production, and custodian of the lighting and cable inventory. Two real notes.

**[Audiovisual Engineer](./audiovisual-engineer/)** — the contact for audio, video, and projection, and custodian of the AV inventory. Includes the assistant role, which is defined by reference to this one. Three real notes.

## Roles that have no handbook yet

Named so the gaps stay visible. **Assistant Head Electrician** — ⚠️ referenced repeatedly in the HE's own duties (*mentors and supervises the AHE(s), including defining their explicit call schedule*) with no page of its own anywhere. Its AV counterpart exists, so this is a real asymmetry rather than a deliberate omission.

Then, from the role-definition work in ClickUp: **Stage Management · LX Programmer · Assistant Designers · Run Crew · Fly Operator · Box Office and Front of House · Scene Shop training.** Those live as in-progress task records, not as authored duties, so they are a source rather than a migration.

## Where these came from

The two that exist were authored in ClickUp as `URITP Duties and Responsibilities`, version 2026. **That doc set is still there and has not been changed.** This is a parallel copy while the approach proves out.

⚠️ **The source carries the department's own frame, and it shows.** These are written as *appointment terms for a student position* as much as craft: course enrollment, lab attendance, office hours. The durable half (who you answer to, what you must physically lead, what an as-hung archive is) travels to any theatre. The institutional half expires when a course number changes. **They are currently mixed in the same file** — splitting them is an open question in the ClickUp decision log, and both packages will get the same treatment in one pass so they do not diverge.
