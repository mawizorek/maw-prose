# Handbooks

*What a role is on the hook for, over a season. Read by whoever holds it.*

- **[Head Electrician](./head-electrician/)** — the contact for everything lighting and electrical on a production, and custodian of the lighting and cable inventory.
- **[Audiovisual Engineer](./audiovisual-engineer/)** — the contact for audio, video and projection, and custodian of the AV inventory. Includes the assistant role.

How a load-in or a tech week runs is not in here. That is [guides/production-phases](../guides/production-phases/), so every department reads the same one.

## Roles with no handbook yet

**Assistant Head Electrician** is the gap that matters. It is referenced repeatedly in the HE's own duties — *mentors and supervises the AHE(s), including defining their explicit call schedule* — and has no page anywhere, while its AV counterpart is written. That is an asymmetry, not a choice.

Then, from the role-definition work in ClickUp: Stage Management, LX Programmer, Assistant Designers, Run Crew, Fly Operator, Box Office and Front of House, Scene Shop training. Those exist as in-progress task records rather than authored duties.

## A note on the source

These were authored in ClickUp as `URITP Duties and Responsibilities`, version 2026, and that set is unchanged.

They carry the department's frame, and it shows: course enrollment, lab attendance, office hours. The durable half — who you answer to, what you must physically lead, what an as-hung archive is — travels to any theatre. The institutional half expires when a course number changes. They are currently in the same file.
