# Audiovisual Engineer

*The map for this package. Written for whoever takes the job next, an assistant stepping up mid-season, or a designer who needs to know what the AVE actually owns. Source: `URITP Duties and Responsibilities`, version 2026. Content last checked: 2026-07-30.*

The Audiovisual Engineer is the contact person for all audio, video, and projection requirements on a production, and the custodian of the AV inventory while that assignment runs. **One role, two systems, and the second one is the reason this job is harder than it looks:** audio and video are different disciplines with different gear, different designers, and different failure modes, and one person holds both.

## What is here

**[The job](./the-job.md)** — who you answer to, who you supervise, what you have to enroll in and attend, and where you have to physically be.

**[Deliverables](./deliverables.md)** — what you owe and roughly when. Paperwork in, feasibility out, and the production archive at the end.

**[The assistant](./assistant.md)** — the AAVE role, which is defined almost entirely by reference to this one.

## What is not here

**How to run a load-in, a sound check, or a strike** — those are production phases, and they live in [guides/production-phases](../../guides/production-phases/) so that every department reads the same one.

**Venue specifics** — amp and rack locations, tie lines, snake runs, mix positions. Those belong to the room: see [venues](../../venues/). ⚠️ Smith has no `audio.md` yet, and that is a named gap.

**Anything signed** — completed forms, dated acknowledgments, crew assignments. FileMaker holds records.
