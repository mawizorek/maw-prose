# Audiovisual Engineer

*Written for whoever takes the job next, an assistant stepping up mid-season, or a designer who needs to know what the AVE owns. Source: `URITP Duties and Responsibilities`, version 2026; checked 2026-07-30.*

The Audiovisual Engineer is the contact person for all audio, video and projection requirements on a production, and the custodian of the AV inventory while that assignment runs. One role, two systems, and the second one is why this job is harder than it looks: audio and video are different disciplines with different gear, different designers and different failure modes, and one person holds both.

- **[The job](./the-job.md)** — who you answer to, who you supervise, what you have to enroll in and attend, and where you have to be.
- **[Deliverables](./deliverables.md)** — what you owe and roughly when.
- **[The assistant](./assistant.md)** — the AAVE role, defined almost entirely by reference to this one.

## What is not here

How a load-in, a sound check or a strike runs — that is [guides/production-phases](../../guides/production-phases/).

Venue specifics — amp and rack locations, tie lines, snake runs, mix positions — belong to the room: [venues](../../venues/). Smith has no `audio.md` yet.

Anything signed — completed forms, dated acknowledgments, crew assignments. FileMaker holds records.
