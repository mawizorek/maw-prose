# Art Cue 'Mood' Project

Typically in SPAC w/ Michael helping you program.  

Choose a piece of art, sculpture, photography or the like (you must be able to show it to the class) and determine a single lighting cue inspired by the piece.  

## Submission

*Things with due dates.*  

1. Choice of artwork  
1. Write an artistic statement detailing your insipiration. You should remark on the four functions of light as they relate to your inspiration and discuss how you intend to use the four qualities of lights to accomplish a light cue inspired by your cue.  
1. Light cue programming time / save file.
1. Presentation in class with brief talkback with class afterwards.

### Blind Inspiration Statement

| field | contents |
|---|---|
| Due | [tbd]{.gap} |
| Total Points | [10]{.conf} |

**Instructions:**

Write an artistic statement detailing your insipiration. You should remark on the four functions of light as they relate to your inspiration and discuss how you intend to use the four qualities of lights to accomplish a light cue inspired by your cue.  

This could focus on:

    - The attributes of light and how you used the qualities of light to achieve a similar affect  

    - What elements of the artowrk inspired your implementation of the same theme?  

    - What similar contrasts did you choose to make between the artwork and your cue?  

#### Criteria

| Points Avaialble | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 4 | Consideration for each attribute of light. |
| 4 | Consideration for each quality of light. |
| 2 | Connected to choice of artwork. |
