# Master profile

*Michael's resume source. Content last checked 10 October 2026 against the Work Experience and Production History lists in ClickUp and the signed University of Rochester offer letter.*

Nothing in this package is sent to anyone as written. A resume in `../resumes/` is cut from it: pick a summary, pick the bullets tagged for the lane, set the dates from ClickUp. If a line is not here, it does not go on a resume, which is the whole point of having one place to look.

- **[Summaries](./summaries.md)** - the two or three opening lines, one version per job lane.
- **[Bullet bank](./bullet-bank.md)** - every achievement line, each tagged with the lanes it serves and the role it belongs to.

Dates, employers, show credits and credentials are records, so they stay in ClickUp: Work Experience for jobs, Production History for credits, Trainings | Certificates | Unions for courses and targets. A credential on the Trainings list that is a target rather than something earned never appears on a resume.

The University appointment is titled Senior Lecturer/Production Manager on every resume.

Bios, long and short, live in `../bios/` (D-045).

**Not written yet:** summaries for the drafting, audio and safety lanes. Write each when an application first needs it, from the bullets already here.
