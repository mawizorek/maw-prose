# Program bio

## Copy

Michael A Wizorek (he/him) is a Senior Lecturer in Theater and the Production Manager of the University of Rochester International Theatre Program, and an avid theater-maker who finds particular enjoyment in the creation and production of new works and accessible theater. Select credits: *Oedipus* (Projection Designer; URITP), *What I Know, Now* (Production Stage Manager; Bay Street Theater), *The Crucible* (Production Stage Manager; Bay Street Theater), *Hedwig and the Angry Inch* (Lighting Designer; University of Rochester), *Singin' in the Rain* (Production Electrician; The Ogunquit Playhouse). He is endlessly grateful for the constant support of his friends, mentors, and family.

## Where it goes

Playbills and show programs. If the show the program is for is one of the five credits, swap it for the next strongest credit; the program's own listing already carries it.
