# Faculty appointments

*How the University employs and re-employs its non-tenure faculty, and what a review cycle asks of you. Read when a contract is coming up. Sources: Appendix II, Contract-Based Faculty Guidelines, from Faculty Governance's Rules and Regulations, and the University Faculty Handbook as updated January 2025. Content last checked 21 September 2026.*

The University runs several parallel appointment ladders, and the one you are on decides what your review measures. That is the whole reason these notes exist: the ladders sound interchangeable, they are not, and the difference shows up as which parts of your job the review is allowed to look at.

Everything here covers the School of Arts & Sciences and the Hajim School of Engineering & Applied Sciences. Theatre sits inside English, which sits inside Arts & Sciences, so it applies.

- **[The tracks](./the-tracks.md)** - the four appointment families, how a rank on one maps to a rank on another, what conversion between them involves, and what neither governing document provides for the teaching track.
- **[Reappointment](./reappointment.md)** - the two review processes, who starts them and when, what you submit, who reads it, and what happens if a decision is contested.

**The single most useful thing in this package:** the detailed reappointment machinery is written for the instructional-track ranks. If an appointment is teaching-track, which is to say lecturer, senior lecturer or full-time instructor, neither Appendix II nor the Handbook supplies a reappointment process, a notification guarantee, or an evaluation rubric for it. The offer letter may be the governing instrument instead. Establish which document governs before working to any deadline, because the two answers are months apart.

**Not written yet:** `promotion.md` - the promotion process is a separate machine from reappointment, with its own timeline, its own materials, and a requirement for three confidential internal letters. Appendix II Section III covers it and nobody has needed it here yet.

## Where the raw material is

The source documents are Appendix II under Rules and Regulations on the Faculty Governance site, the University Faculty Handbook, and two referee-letter templates for the instructional-track promotion cases. A copy of Appendix II and both templates is attached to the Contract Re-appointment task in ClickUp, and the Handbook is attached to the original application task.

The University revises both documents, and the Handbook carries a cumulative summary of changes at the end that is worth checking first because it dates every revision. Anything read here should be checked against the live page before it is relied on in a submission, since a procedural rule that changed quietly is exactly the kind of thing that costs a deadline. Note also the Handbook's own precedence rules: it does not supersede a school's own faculty rules, and where it conflicts with the University's Human Resources policies, those personnel policies govern.

Your own appointment facts, meaning dates, rank, track, and what you submitted when, are records rather than reference, so they live in ClickUp and not here. This package tells you how the system works; it does not know where you sit in it.
