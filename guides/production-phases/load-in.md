# Load-in

*Hang, cable, dimmer check, focus. The days where everyone is on their feet and every decision is expensive. Content last checked: 2026-07-30 (the focus section only; everything else is still to write).*

## Staffing a focus session: three jobs, not two

**Split the room three ways.** One person drives the console. One person owns the focus charts and interprets them. The Head Electrician floats between them.

**Chart-reading is a distinct job from running the board, and that is the part people get wrong.** Reading a designer's chart while also patching and pushing faders is how a focus session stalls: the console operator ends up doing two things badly and the room waits. Give the charts to someone whose only job is the charts.

**The HE floats.** Making sure the console has what it needs, and that the chart reading is unambiguous. Not on the board, not on the charts, watching the seam between them.

**If the charts arrive before the session, the person who will interpret them reads them cold first and takes their questions to the designer.** Before anyone is up a ladder. A question asked at the ladder costs the whole room; the same question asked three days earlier costs an email.

*Source: how this was run on a 2026 mainstage where the designer delivered focus charts in advance. A practice, not a policy — and written to the seats rather than the people who filled them, so it survives the crew turning over.*
