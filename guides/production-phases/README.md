# Production phases

*What happens during each phase of a production. Followed in the moment, by whoever is running it. Content last checked: 2026-07-30.*

A production moves through the same sequence every time, and the sequence is the industry's, not the department's: **preseason → paperwork prep → load-in → tech → show run → strike**, with inventory as a standing duty rather than a phase. Every theatre loads in and strikes, which is why these notes travel and a semester calendar does not.

## What exists

**[Load-in](./load-in.md)** — one real section, on how to staff a focus session. Everything else is still to write.

## What does not exist yet

## Where the raw material is

The richest source is `URITP Production ▸ EVENTS | CALENDARS` in ClickUp, which holds a page per production event, some substantial and some a few lines. **The intended way these get written: after a production, from its postmortem, while it is still fresh.** That is the update loop, and it is the only mechanism that has ever kept this kind of document alive.

⚠️ **Per-show postmortem notes stay in ClickUp.** They are records, they name people, and they assess them. What comes here is the durable practice with the names taken out.
