# Updating Production Calendars
> Track when things happen. The schedule backbone shared across platforms.

## What It Is
The master production schedule: rehearsals, tech, performances, deadlines. Lives primarily on Google Calendar, mirrored/audited in ClickUp.

## Who Maintains
- **Google Calendar:** Nigel (owner), PM (primary editor)
- **ClickUp mirror:** Michael (private sync/audit surface)
- **Info sheet dates section:** PM

## Where It Lives
| Platform | Location |
|----------|----------|
| Google Calendar | Public Production Calendar (shared, all mainstage) |
| ClickUp | Task dates + Calendar view (private audit mirror) |
| Info Sheet | "Key Dates" + "Other Dates" sections (Dropbox Word/PDF) |

## Event Types
- Rehearsals (read-throughs, stumble-throughs, runs)
- Tech week (load-in, hang, focus, quiet time, tech, dress)
- Performances (opening, runs, pickup)
- Design deadlines (prelim/final per department)
- Administrative (auditions, callbacks, meet & greet, strike)

## Update Triggers
- Date change from director, Nigel, or venue
- Addition/cancellation of rehearsal or performance
- Shift in tech schedule
- Academic calendar conflict (break, Meliora/Dandelion)

## Cross-References
When you update calendar dates, also check:
- **Info sheet** (Key Dates + Other Dates sections)
- **Crew calls** (daily schedule pulls from master calendar)
- **ClickUp task dates** (mirror events for private audit)
- **Google Calendar event** (if editing from ClickUp side)

## Audit Checklist
- [ ] Google Cal events match info sheet "Key Dates"
- [ ] Design deadlines on Google Cal match info sheet "Other Dates"
- [ ] No orphaned ClickUp date-tasks without matching Cal event
- [ ] Academic calendar conflicts noted/avoided
- [ ] All events have correct time, location, and description
