# Guides

*How to do one thing, read at the moment of need. Content last checked: 2026-07-30.*

**A standard is cited; a guide is followed.** If someone would point at it to settle an argument about the right way to do something, it is a [standard](../standards/). If someone would read it start to finish while doing the task, it belongs here.

## What is here

**[Production phases](./production-phases/)** — what happens during each phase of a production, in what order, and what bites you. Followed by whoever is running that phase, whatever department they are from.

## Why phases are guides and not handbooks

A **handbook** belongs to a person in a role: *what am I on the hook for over a season.* A **phase guide** belongs to the phase: *it is load-in morning, what happens now.*

That distinction is not pedantry, it is the thing that stops duplication. Electrics, audio, video, scenic and stage management all live through the same load-in. If load-in lived inside each role's handbook there would be five load-in documents drifting apart. **One phase guide, cited by every role that touches it.**
