# Venues — moved

**Venue documentation does not live in this repo.** Ruled by Michael, 2026-09-10: *"Any info about the venue should go into my URITP notes and not in my Vectorworks prose notes. They are entirely different!"*

The rule: **a rule is a standard, an instance is a venue fact.** How a Vectorworks file is built is portable craft and lives in [standards/vectorworks](../standards/vectorworks/). What is true about a particular room — its layer list, its class inventory, its load limits, its reference planes — belongs to the building, and lives with the URITP notes.

The Smith Theatre content that was here has moved to the URITP documentation, where the pages were already scaffolded and waiting for it.

## What is safe to delete, and what is not

🗑️ **The tombstoned files are ready to go:** this README, `smith-theatre/README.md`, `smith-theatre/layers.md`, `smith-theatre/classes.md`, `smith-theatre/electrics.md`. Their content has moved or was empty. They are left in place only so nothing reads as lost.

⚠️ **`smith-theatre/classes/` is NOT tombstoned and must not be swept up.** It holds `walls/east-wall.md`, `north-wall.md`, `south-wall.md` and `west-wall.md` — authored by Michael on 2026-08-17, headings only, untouched by this pass.

They carry an open question rather than a verdict: a wall is architecture, not an object category, so filing wall documentation under `classes/` may be a placement slip — or these may be per-wall **systems** notes (the unistrut levels, the hung panels) that landed one level off. Either way they are venue content by the ruling above, so their destination is the URITP notes. **Michael decides what they were meant to be before anything happens to them.**
